How routes and hybrid systems are assigned

Apply the rules in order: geometric reuse of identity-specific appearance → rendering; a noise-independent causal visual state with a local output head → visual AR; a temporal backbone repeatedly denoising the current visual sample → diffusion; otherwise, an adversarial feed-forward visual generator → GAN. The primary route is a reading index. Appearance representation, temporal state, training, and personalization remain separate annotations.

MIDAS and Body of Her
The causal backbone supplies a fixed next-frame state to a local iterative head: visual AR.
X-Streamer
The Actor’s visual backbone receives current noisy latents and the diffusion timestep at each denoising step: diffusion, despite causal chunk emission.
OmniResponse
Autoregressive text and facial-coefficient prediction coordinates conversational responses. A separate portrait renderer produces the images, so it appears in AR browsing as a controller while retaining its rendering-primary count.
StyleAvatar and LivePortrait
StyleAvatar queries facial UV appearance under geometric control; LivePortrait warps a source feature volume. Both are rendering-primary, with learned image synthesis retained as a secondary component.

A motion controller does not become a visual AR method merely because it is autoregressive. Foundational or unresolved references can remain in the reading collection without contributing to a primary route count. The appearance owner need not be the timing bottleneck or the source of a temporal failure.

Five recurring patterns for stability

These operations recur in streaming visual generation. Their effect depends on the task, retained state, and evaluation duration.

L1 · Persistent identity anchors

Retain a reference image, appearance asset, or designated latent. Identity retention and the ability to follow intended changes both require testing. Explore identity anchors.

L2 · Bounded multiscale memory

Keep recent and selected or compressed older temporal state under documented capacities and update rules. A detail-retrieval bank, codec history, or recent-only KV window does not alone qualify. Explore documented multiscale memory.

L3 · Position re-indexing

Reassign temporal indices as a cache advances. This addresses positional extrapolation without recovering evicted content or correcting appearance errors. Explore documented position policies.

L4 · History-robust training

L4-G uses model-generated histories; L4-C corrupts ground-truth histories. These expose a model to different error distributions. Neither guarantees distribution matching for arbitrarily long deployment.

L5 · Explicit boundary-state transfer

Transfer a designated motion or boundary representation between chunks. Ordinary conditioning on earlier frames is insufficient for this label. Transfer can preserve continuity or propagate an error. Explore documented transfer mechanisms.
